Our Core Expertise

Leveraging nano-chemical and mechanical innovations to outperform conventional methods across three high-impact domains.

Brownfield Wellbore Rejuvenation

From precise wellbore diagnosis to advanced rodless pumping and nano-chemical stimulation, we rejuvenate brownfield wells faster, cleaner, and more cost effective than traditional work overs.

Up to 40% lower mobilization costs compared to conventional work overs
Rapid production restart within 72 hours, minimizing downtime
Smart cloud based control with real time monitoring and predictive analytics

Pipeline Asset Rejuvenation

From progressive pigging and nano chemical de-gelling to in-line inspection and GIS integrity management, we restore pipeline capacity, extend lifespan, and ensure reliable long‑term performance.

Progressive pigging with staged mechanical tools for thorough cleaning
Nano chemical de-gelling to break gelled crude, lower viscosity, and improve flow
High resolution in-line inspection detecting corrosion and defects with MFL and geometry tools
GIS integrity management for digital mapping and long term monitoring of pipeline health

Next-Gen Sludge Oil Recovery

By combining closed mechanical cleaning with nano‑chemical separation and three phase processing, we maximize crude recovery while minimizing waste and ensuring eco compliant disposal.

Safe closed system operation preventing emissions (COW)
Maximized crude recovery through advanced separation
Environmentally compliant disposal of minimized sludge residues
Reduced long term maintenance burden for sustainable operations
